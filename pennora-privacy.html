Privacy Policy

Pennora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

Pennora ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal expense and income diary. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: Pennora stores everything on your device and does not collect, transmit, or sell your data.

Summary
  • All your entries, notes, categories and settings stay on your device.
  • We have no servers and no account system. We never receive your financial data.
  • Backups are files you control. When your app has a PIN, backups are encrypted with it.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no third-party tracking.

1. Information the app stores

Pennora stores the data you enter — transaction amounts, titles, notes, dates, categories, payment methods and tags — in a private database on your device. Your app settings (currency, theme, budget, backup preferences) and your optional lock settings are also stored locally. This information is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

2. Information we collect

None. Pennora has no backend servers, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising libraries. We do not collect personal information, usage statistics, device identifiers, or location data.

3. App lock (PIN & biometrics)

You may optionally protect the app with a PIN and/or biometric unlock (fingerprint or face). Your PIN is stored on the device as a salted cryptographic hash, and a copy used for backup encryption is sealed using the Android Keystore (hardware-backed where available). Biometric verification is performed by your device's operating system; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint or face data.

4. Backups and transfers

Pennora can create backup files so you can move your data to another device or keep a copy. These backups are written to your device's public Downloads/Pennora folder and, optionally, shared by you through apps you choose.

  • Encryption. If your app has a PIN set, backups are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2). A backup copied to another device cannot be read or restored without that PIN.
  • Your control. Backup files live in your device storage. If you share or move a backup, it travels only to the destination you choose. We never receive it.
  • Restore. Restoring an encrypted backup requires entering its PIN.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Biometric — to offer fingerprint/face unlock for the optional app lock.
  • Microphone (record audio) — only when you tap the microphone button to dictate a diary note. Speech is processed by your device's own speech recognition; the app stores only the resulting text you keep.
  • Notifications — to show backup or reminder notifications, where applicable.
  • Internet — used only by Google's ML Kit to download an on-device translation language model the first time you translate a note. Your note text is translated on the device and is not sent to us.
  • Storage (older Android versions only) — to save and read backup files in your public Downloads folder. On newer Android versions this works without a storage permission.

6. On-device translation (Google ML Kit)

The optional "Translate" feature uses Google's ML Kit on-device translation. The first time you use a language, its model is downloaded to your device. After that, translation runs offline on your device. Model downloads are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Your note content is not transmitted to us.

7. Children's privacy

Pennora is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children (and in fact collect no personal information from anyone).

8. Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by deleting entries within the app, deleting backup files from your Downloads folder, or uninstalling the app (which removes the app's private database). Backup files you have copied elsewhere must be deleted by you in those locations.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, within the app or store listing.
